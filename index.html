HOW IT WORKS / TIPS
  1. Paste your Gemini API key and pick a target language.
  2. Press Start. Your browser asks what to share — choose This Tab (or the tab/window playing the stream) and tick Share tab audio.
  3. The stream audio is sent to Gemini 3.5 Live Translate; captions and translated voice come back in a few seconds.
  4. Avoiding echo: for the cleanest translated voice, open the livestream in a separate tab and share that tab, so the translated voice playing here isn’t re-captured.
