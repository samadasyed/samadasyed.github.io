HOW IT WORKS / TIPS
  1. Pick a target language and press Start. Your browser asks what to share — choose a Chrome Tab and tick Also share tab audio.
  2. The stream audio is sent to Gemini 3.5 Live Translate; live captions and the translated voice come back a few seconds behind.
  3. Captions off the embedded player: share This Tab and turn off “play translated voice”. Captions stay live and there’s no echo.
  4. Translated voice too: open the stream in a separate tab and share that tab. The voice plays here, the original plays there, so nothing is re-captured. (A cross-origin YouTube player can’t be tapped directly — tab sharing is the way in.)
